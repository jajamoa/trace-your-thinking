# Participant UI: Living the Dream direction

Visual reference: [Nintendo's Tomodachi Life: Living the Dream](https://www.nintendo.com/sg/switch/blfga/index.html).

The participant screens use sky blue, lime, sunshine yellow, rounded white panels, dimensional buttons, conversational labels, and an original neighborhood illustration. The study identity, English content, Prolific flow, desktop participation requirement, and research disclosures remain in place. Administrative screens keep their existing theme.
